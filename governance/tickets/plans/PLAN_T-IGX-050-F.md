## 1. Purpose

Give the final ARCH-FREEZE ENTRY check a mechanical list. `docs/project/maintainers/qualification/arch_freeze_program/RM499_ENTRY_MAP.md` lists every mechanical entry condition of the roadmap ARCH-FREEZE row (L499) and of its Path B row (L267), quotes each condition verbatim, and ends each row either `OPEN` with the plan node that closes it or `EVIDENCED` with a `path@sha`. It covers in particular: roadmap OPEN/BLOCKED = 0 including the two TRACE-X rows at L401 and L410 (`READY FOR AUDIT`); Harness invariant = 0; Top-Tier gap = 0; unclassified compatibility seam = 0; unversioned frozen contract = 0; reopened stages = 0; replay findings = 0; the 7 VBN artefacts; and SKILL-X, TOOL-LIFE-X, DIST-SCALE-X, EBH-7 and ROADMAP-REPLAY-X independence. It adds an interpretation note for the post-freeze PLANNED and BLOCKED rows.

The map is a planning record. It records no PASS, closes no stage and verifies nothing independently. `EVIDENCED` means only that a tracked record at the cited path states the condition.
