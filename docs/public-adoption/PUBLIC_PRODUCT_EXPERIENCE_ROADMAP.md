## Purpose

This roadmap changes the repository experience from technically correct documentation into a **product-first experience** that is:

- immediately understandable;
- visually concrete;
- easy to navigate;
- easy to try;
- honest about maturity;
- useful to several reader groups.

This is **not** permission to exaggerate claims or fabricate product visuals, screenshots, testimonials, or validation results.

## First-contact success contract

These are **target experience gates**. They define measurable outcomes for future public documentation and sessions. They are **not currently claimed** as achieved.

| Time gate | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| **15 seconds** | Understand that Intergrax is a serious foundation for governed AI applications and that LKW is the primary product path. |
| **60 seconds** | Understand the problem, the LKW role, the current maturity, and one next action. |
| **5 minutes** | See a concrete truthful product result or product tour. |
| **15 minutes** | Reach the first useful LKW result in a supported evaluation environment — **after PX-3 provides the actual path**. This target is **not currently claimed** as achieved. |
| **30 minutes** | Inspect citations or evidence and understand the difference between product trial and platform proof. |
| **60 minutes** | Choose and begin an appropriate product, builder, architecture, or partner path. |

Actual completion of these gates requires later external sessions (PX-13, PX-14) and accepted phase delivery. No fictional session results may be recorded.

## Frozen principles

1. Product first, platform second.
2. LKW is the primary public product CTA.
3. Token Optimization is a secondary capability CTA.
4. Product trial and platform evaluation are different routes.
5. `echo.basic` and `lab_application` are not primary public product demonstrations.
6. Echo/lab may remain advanced platform smoke or maintainer diagnostics.
7. Value and concrete outcome appear before detailed limitations.
8. Limitations remain visible and truthful.
9. Public readers do not need internal task IDs.
10. Public pages use progressive disclosure.
11. Every major reader path has one primary next action.
12. No fabricated UI, testimonials, sessions, or product results.
13. A certification runbook must not replace a product tour.
14. Current behavior and future targets must be clearly separated.
15. New public content must have a named audience and purpose.
