# DECISION_DELIBERATION — Council and disagreement

**Parent hub:** [`DECISION_DELIBERATION.md`](../DECISION_DELIBERATION.md)

## Council as DecisionStrategy

Council orchestrates parallel participant proposals under Nexus — **no Council Runtime**.

## Proposals and branches

Each participant may emit candidates; branches remain in version lineage.

## Disagreement artifact

Structured capture of positions, alternatives, and evidence refs — preserved through synthesis.

## Synthesis

Produces a candidate Decision Version for verification — majority vote must not erase material dissent.

## Adjudication interface

Deadlock or irresolvable conflict routes to adjudication or `UNRESOLVED` — not hidden tie-break.
