## What is Intergrax?

Intergrax is a reusable governed foundation and application operating layer for specialized AI applications. It helps teams compose policy, knowledge, evidence, integration, and execution boundaries around a concrete workflow.

## How is it different from SaaS, a framework, or retrieval tooling?

It is not a finished SaaS, a retrieval toolkit, or a universal replacement for other approaches. Its distinction is the responsibility boundary: reusable governed mechanisms support an application whose product workflow, permissions, deployment, and acceptance remain application-owned. See the [bounded category comparison](WHY_INTERGRAX.md#where-intergrax-fits).

## What is LKW?

Local Knowledge Workspace is the **Primary Product Proof**: **Backend Product Alpha / MVP**, **PARTIAL**. It demonstrates a bounded indexed-knowledge workflow with a grounded answer, source reference, and persisted Ask run. [PROOFS](../proofs/PROOFS.md) owns current evidence status.

## What is proven today?

Current claim status belongs to [PROOFS](../proofs/PROOFS.md). It separates implemented mechanisms, bounded proof, partial capability, and claims that are not established. It does not claim finished SaaS, production readiness, real-user validation, or commercial validation.

## Is Hybrid Ask proven?

Partly, with boundaries. The [Product Quick Start](../../../applications/local_workspace_application/docs/product/QUICKSTART.md) exercises indexed **Ask V1** — it is not the Hybrid Ask certification path. Separate accepted evidence demonstrates the bounded indexed branch through Hybrid Ask; see [PROOFS](../proofs/PROOFS.md) and [LKW Platform Proof](../../../applications/local_workspace_application/docs/proof/LKW_PLATFORM_PROOF.md). Mixed indexed + authorized-live Hybrid Ask in one answer is **not proven**; this does not imply general production readiness or complete live-provider access.

## Is Intergrax open source?

No. It is **source-available** under the [Intergrax Evaluation and Collaboration License 1.0](../../../LICENSE). Public source access does not itself grant open-source, production, commercial, hosting, or redistribution rights.

## Can a company evaluate Intergrax?

Yes, internal non-production Evaluation by a commercial organization may be permitted under the [LICENSE](../../../LICENSE), subject to its exact terms and a controlled Evaluation Environment. Use the [Evaluation Guide](../builders/EVALUATION_GUIDE.md) for the method.

## Can I use it in production or commercially?

The [LICENSE](../../../LICENSE) controls this boundary. Production Use and Commercial Use require separate explicit written permission or agreement; a document route or request does not grant permission. For a practical request route, see [Collaboration](../community/COLLABORATION.md).
