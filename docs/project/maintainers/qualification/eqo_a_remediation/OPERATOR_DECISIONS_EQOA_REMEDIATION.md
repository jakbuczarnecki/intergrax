## Overriding conditions (binding)

C1. Historical certifications without preserved evidence must not be presented as independently verified. Operator acceptance does not replace technical evidence. A TOOL-LIFE-X re-audit is required and the labels of the other stages must be corrected. Every evidence gap needs an ARCH-FREEZE impact assessment. If other stages need a re-audit to meet mandatory criteria, that necessity must be shown explicitly.

C2. Documenting a seam and freezing its allowlist is not automatic debt acceptance. Every seam is assessed against canonical ownership, boundary isolation, security, tenant isolation and execution authority. Violations of critical invariants are ARCH-FREEZE blockers unless a formally permitted resolution exists under the canonical roadmap.

C3. PRODUCT/STRICT must be fail-closed. Silent fallbacks may remain only where applicable contracts explicitly allow them and they do not weaken production guarantees. Code fixes run after the appropriate PDC window and before ARCH-FREEZE when they are blocking requirements. Required: negative tests, regression, and an independent audit at the exact SHA.

C4. One consolidated, traceable Enterprise Debt & Exceptions Register and a qualification index, without duplicating canonical domain ownership. Every finding carries owner, severity, source, SHA, FRZ/ARCH-FREEZE impact, disposition and closure condition.

C5. Campaign A stays an audit campaign, not a remediation campaign. No production code is modified within it. Findings that need a fix get a separate execution plan, appropriate qualification and independent certification.

C6. Results are not optimised for a green status. The optimisation target is factual, evidence-based Enterprise quality and ARCH-FREEZE readiness.
