## 1. How to read this map

- **Condition text** is the roadmap's own wording, quoted verbatim with the markdown emphasis removed (a script asserts each quote against the roadmap at the base SHA). Where a row prints a condition twice, it is listed once and the repeat is noted. Rows RM499-M10.1 to M10.7 and RM499-M25a to M25f were split from one list in the roadmap row and repeat that list's shared prefix ("mandatory vendor/backend evidence artifacts accepted on current HEAD:" and "exact-freeze-SHA enterprise audit:"). The bracketed "(evidence specification 2.x)" in the M10 rows and the whole SQLite annex row RM499-M10.A are the map's own words, not roadmap text.
- **Status** has two values. `OPEN`: no record shows the condition holds at the FREEZE SHA, or the plan or a diagnostic says it does not; the closing node is named. `EVIDENCED`: a tracked record at the cited `path@sha` states the condition (for a stage, the roadmap row carries the label); it does not assert that the record is correct or independent, nor that the condition still holds at the FREEZE SHA. The ENTRY check re-verifies every row at the FREEZE SHA. At this base: 41 rows `OPEN`, 5 rows `EVIDENCED`.
- **Closing nodes** are nodes of MEP-AF2 v2.1 (`T-AF2-*`, `T-B7-*`, `T-B8-*`) and of the EQO-A WP-12 DAG (`PT-*`), as published in [`MEP-AF2_v2.1.md`](./MEP-AF2_v2.1.md) and [`WP12_DAG.md`](./WP12_DAG.md). The map invents no node; where the plan names none, the cell says so and the gap is listed in section 6.
- **Diagnostic inputs** (section 7) are non-certifying session measurements. A row that cites one says "Diagnostic"; such a citation is never the basis of an `EVIDENCED` status.
- **OPEN is not a roadmap status.** Roadmap section 2.2 lists CLOSED, CURRENT, READY FOR AUDIT, BLOCKED, QUEUED, PLANNED and FINAL / MANDATORY. This map reads "OPEN" in the entry conditions as "not closed" (observation O-7).
