## 1. What this document is, and is not

- It is a **qualification of residual admissibility for ARCH-FREEZE**. It records, per residual, what the PDC documents state, what was checked statically at the baseline, what the plan assigns, and whether deferral can be allowed.
- It is **not a re-certification of PDC-X**. PDC-X closed on 2026-11-16 as a scoped closure (`PASS_SCOPED`); closing PDC-X does not make its residuals acceptable for a frozen architecture, and this file does not reopen or re-rate PDC-X.
- **No row is accepted or closed by this document.** Final acceptance of every row belongs to the W7 independent audit (T-B7-ECAP-2C). The implementer of this document is not an auditor.
- Dispositions in column `ARCH_FREEZE_IMPACT` are quoted from plan section E with attribution (`DS-EXC: ...`); they are the Operator's recorded recommended options, not claims of this document.

### Reading guide

- `PRODUCTION_REACHABILITY` uses three marks. `PDC_STATED`: a quotation or paraphrase of a PDC document, not re-verified. `STATIC_VERIFIED @7960ae7f6 path:line`: checked with `git grep` or a file read at the baseline in this session. `TO_BE_MEASURED (T-AF2-CLOSED-WORLD)`: not yet measured; the closed-world engine re-measures reachability at HEAD, and for E3/E4-class items it runs first (DP-33). A static check shows only what its sentence says; it never proves the absence of a path unless it says so.
- `DEFER_ALLOWED` is `no` for every tenant, security, fail-closed, contract and execution-protection row on a reachable path (plan E policy). A dormant (NC) item is acceptable only with a non-composition gate that fails closed **and** a `NOT_COMPOSED` mark. Missing features follow DP-30: raise until built, plus an exception stated in the freeze documents.
- `REMEDIATION` names the plan node and, where the plan gives one, the ticket (`T-IGX-050-*` W0-W2, `T-IGX-051-*` RW product train, `T-IGX-052-*` W4-W8). PT-* nodes keep their EQO-A names.
- `OWNER` is the owner the PDC register names; the delivery owner is the node in `REMEDIATION`. `AUDITOR` and `FINAL_ACCEPTANCE` are the same in every row on purpose.
- Line numbers in PDC quotations refer to the files at the baseline SHA. Several `path:line` cites in the PDC register have drifted since it was written; where this file noticed that, it says so in the row.
