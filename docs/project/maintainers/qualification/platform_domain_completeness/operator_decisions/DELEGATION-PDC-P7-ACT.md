## Amendment 01 check (done before this record was written)

- R-6 lets the Operator commission a separate session to certify in the Operator's name; a chat commission is sufficient (section 1.2). Section 0 strikes S-3: no decision class is excluded, expressly including IIAC policy activation. R-4 even lets a designated session be the certifier of the same stage; this record keeps the roles apart (stricter, not looser). The delegation therefore permits items (b) and (c) below. S-1 is struck, so this record is a transcription and needs no Operator signature line.
- Tooling limit (section 6a): the activation gate reads the plain line `Approved IIAC policy activation IIAC-2026-11-05.1: operator @ <timestamp>` (regex `_SIGNATURE`) and cannot tell who wrote it; the protocol's "Policy activation" item 3 says "operator" literally. Under S-2 the plain form is permitted where a validator accepts only that form **if the commission names it: this commission names it** for the activation line (item (c)). The disclosure that the line was written by a delegate is carried by this record and by the commit message, and the line is not claimed to be Operator-written. No text of the protocol or of the gate is changed by this record.
- Standing answer 6 requires the delegate to confirm first that the protocol and Amendment 01 permit delegated activation lines; the activation delegate repeats that check itself and writes nothing if it fails.

## Scope of the delegation

**(a) Signing the plan of T-IGX-040-B.** The ticket and plan were written by an implementer-lane session and merged unsigned by PR 550 (merge `4835bae8e`; plan digest `c48a13bb8895d1cf4ab5bcceaa6c79307790cb47e9bed85bb8a9b4681ad6893b`); this record does not change them. A dedicated delegate session (new session, dedicated solely to this signature, not a continuation, not an implementation session, not an implementer of any PDC batch) may write `Approved: operator-delegate @ <ISO8601>` and `Delegation: <this file>@<commit sha>` in `governance/tickets/T-IGX-040-B.md` only, after confirming that: the plan digest recomputed with `scripts/governance/plan_digest.py` equals the digest in the ticket; the write set of the plan is exactly one path, the IIAC protocol file, lines 4 and 91 (the ticket's own lifecycle and approval lines are bookkeeping, not write set), and nothing more; the plan keeps every line of the protocol other than lines 4 and 91 byte for byte unchanged; the planned text changes no rule, gate, role or ACT-N* check, touches no activation record and no verifier; no new registry, scheduler, engine, trust, policy or audit authority is introduced; no path lies under the hotspots `intergrax/contracts/` or `intergrax/applications/_shared/`; the plan states that it is not a certification verdict and not an activation. If one check fails the delegate writes nothing and reports.

**(b) Delta audit and certifier publication.** The orchestrator may start, autonomously (Standing answer 7):

- **ONE independent IIAC delta-audit session.** A clean session, never an implementer of any PDC batch, never a delegate that signed a PDC plan or line, never fixing what it audits. It audits the delta `2d3eceb6cf7e93dccd4d96919319a6c969b4444b..<baseline>` where `<baseline>` is the tip of `development` after the merge of the T-IGX-040-B implementation PR (further docs-only descendants are allowed; no product path and no material governance path changes between the baseline and the activation). The delta contains the product paths reported by the gate (138 at `622a61b96`, to be recomputed by the auditor) and the protocol file, the only material governance path expected in it (the auditor checks the list in `MATERIAL_GOVERNANCE_PATHS`). The auditor writes the audit report, scope, test matrix and findings register and the manifest data it is responsible for, and states a verdict. It never certifies.
- **ONE certifier session.** A different clean session (not the auditor, not an implementer, not the signer of (a), not the activation delegate of (c); stricter than R-3 requires). It validates the audit set and publishes, as byte-preserving copies under `docs/project/maintainers/qualification/iiac_evidence/<run-id>/`, the artifacts that `scripts/governance/iiac_activation_gate.py` reads for a new activation: the audit report (`audit_report_path`, its SHA-256 as `session_b_governance_audit_report_digest`); the audit session manifest (`session_b_manifest_path`, `stage_id` `IIAC-GOV-1`, `policy_version` `IIAC-2026-11-05.1`, `verdict` `PASS`, distinct implementation, auditor and certifier session and agent ids, `audit_execution_sha`, `certification_publication_sha`, the digests of report, scope, matrix and findings; its SHA-256 as `session_b_manifest_digest`); the audit scope (`audit_scope_path`), the test matrix (`test_matrix_path`) and the findings register (`findings_path`; no open BLOCKER or CRITICAL finding, ACT-N15); the session handoff record `IIAC_SESSION_HANDOFF_RECORD_*.md` under `docs/project/maintainers/qualification/` (`handoff_record_path`, `handoff_digest`; it names the policy version, the implementation session id and the auditor session id, ACT-N17). It runs `verify_iiac_audit_manifest.py` and `verify_iiac_role_separation.py --strict-product-diff` on the manifest (ACT-N10 companions) and reports the exit codes. The binding fields (`corrective_implementation_sha`, `evidence_publication_sha`, `certification_publication_sha`) are taken as the gate requires (ancestry chain, ACT-N09; `certification_publication_sha` different from the corrective SHA); the orchestrator does not choose them.
- The audit and certifier verdicts are theirs. The orchestrator never edits, softens or re-words them, and never starts a second auditor to obtain a different verdict on the same SHA.

**(c) Activation line.** A separate delegate session (new, dedicated, not the signer of (a), not the auditor, not the certifier, not an implementer) may write, **only after** the preconditions of `DELEGATION-PDC-STANDING.md` answer 6 hold, namely: no product delta between the audit execution SHA and the activation HEAD (ACT-N11), including no change of any material governance path; `verify_iiac_policy_activation.py` preconditions and `verify_iiac_role_separation.py` pass on the published evidence; the P6 evidence is complete; and the audit manifest verdict is `PASS` with the certifier's evidence published. It then writes (1) the operator decision record `docs/project/maintainers/qualification/IIAC_OD_ACTIVATION_*.md` (decision id, `Policy version: IIAC-2026-11-05.1`, `Decision scope: IIAC policy activation`, `Decision status: AUTHORIZED`, `Audit report digest: <digest>`, and the plain line `Approved IIAC policy activation IIAC-2026-11-05.1: operator @ <ISO8601>`, which this commission names as the form to use); and (2) the two-phase publication of `IIAC_POLICY_ACTIVATION_RECORD.json` (phase 1: `IN_FORCE` with empty `effective_from_sha`; phase 2: `effective_from_sha` and `policy_activation_sha` set to the phase-1 commit; Amendment 01 section 4.3). The delegate runs `verify_iiac_policy_activation.py --require-in-force` afterwards and reports the result. It never edits the protocol, the gate, a verifier or the audit evidence, and never closes a stage (ACT-N18). If a precondition fails it writes nothing and reports.

**(d) Merges.** The orchestrating session (the implementer lane of `DELEGATION-PDC-STANDING.md`) merges, with `gh pr merge`, after green CI and a `headRefOid` equal to the verified SHA: the docs-only pull requests of this path (the T-IGX-040-B ticket and plan, the signature, the evidence publication, the SHA ladder row); the T-IGX-040-B implementation pull request; and the pull requests of the activation delegate of (c). Two clarifications. First, authorization 2 of Standing and decision 8 of Wave 2 exclude changes of IIAC protocol or activation files from implementer merges; for the pull requests listed here that exclusion is lifted by the Operator's commission ("implementation and merge", "the whole path") and only for them, and only for a session that did not write the diff. Second, the only red class tolerated at merge is the known IIAC activation red (ACT-N11) until the activation merges; every other red stops the merge.

**(e) Loops.** A FAIL, a verdict the gate does not accept (CONDITIONAL, PARTIAL, PASS_SCOPED) or open findings of the IIAC delta audit follow the 2-loop rule of decision 6 of `DELEGATION-PDC-WAVE2.md`: the finding is repaired in a ticket of the owning domain, a delegate session may sign that plan or its write-set amendment under the limits of answer 4 of `DELEGATION-PDC-STANDING.md`, a fresh auditor re-audits the corrected SHA (the baseline is fixed again; a repair that touches a material governance path or a product path repeats the audit scope), at most two loops per domain. A third loop, a finding of a new kind, a hotspot path, a gate whose meaning would change, a new authority, a verdict that cannot be repaired, or anything not listed in this record returns to the Operator.
